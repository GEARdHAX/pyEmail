# Privacy Policy — pyEmail

pyEmail is a personal, open-source tool that its owner runs on their own computer to send
and track their own outreach email. It is not a hosted service and has no other users.

## What the app accesses
When its owner signs in with Google, pyEmail asks for access to:

- **Gmail (send and read-only):** to send the owner's own emails, and to read reply and
  delivery-failure messages so their status can be recorded.
- **Google Sheets:** to read the owner's own spreadsheet of contacts and to write a status
  value (such as "EMAIL_SENT") back into it.

## What happens to the data
- All processing happens on the owner's own computer.
- Data is stored only in the owner's own Google account and in local files on that computer.
- The app does **not** sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone, and it
  does not use it for advertising or to train any model.
- Open and click tracking uses a small image and redirect link served from the owner's own
  address. It records only that a message was opened or a link was clicked, with a
  de-identified hash of the visitor, not the visitor's name or address.

## Retention and deletion
Data stays until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local files.

## Google API Services User Data Policy
pyEmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.
